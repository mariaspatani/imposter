RULES

  1. Each team must consist of 4 participants.
  2. All team members will receive the main problem statement, while one randomly selected member will secretly receive an additional/different objective.
  3. The impostor must not openly reveal their identity or objective.
  4. Teams must develop a functional solution within the given time limit.
  5. Participants may discuss, plan, and divide tasks freely within their team.
  6. The use of AI tools, internet resources, and pre-existing code will be allowed/restricted according to the event coordinator's guidelines.
  7. Teams will have designated checkpoints where they may discuss their progress and identify the suspected impostor.
  8. The impostor receives points for successfully influencing the team's outcome without being detected.
  9. The team receives points based on:
    • The quality of the technical solution.
    • Correct identification of the impostor.
  10. Any attempt to communicate the secret objective to another team or deliberately violate the event rules may result in disqualification.
  11. Judges' decisions will be final.
